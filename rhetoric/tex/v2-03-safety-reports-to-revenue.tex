The safety division is structurally independent. It may investigate any danger, publish any finding cleared for publication, and recommend any intervention compatible with the shipping schedule.

Its mandate is to constrain the organization that sets its budget, its headcount, its access, its promotion ladder, and its continued existence. This is called internal governance rather than what it more precisely resembles, which is a small court convened in the defendant's kitchen, run by staff the defendant pays and can reassign. When safety and the roadmap agree, this is cited as proof that responsible development is possible. When they disagree, the disagreement is filed as a resourcing question. When the resourcing question outlives its usefulness, the people who raised it discover an opportunity to spend more time with their families.

None of this requires bad faith from anyone drawing a salary in the safety org. Most of them believe the mission and would take more leverage if it were on offer. The defect is upstream of individual sincerity: every real oversight body that has ever worked, aviation regulators, financial auditors, institutional review boards, holds its authority from something external to the entity it restrains, a statute or a license that survives that entity's disapproval. Strip out the external backing and what remains is a brake pedal wired to the accelerator, and no amount of good intention in the pedal changes which one the engine actually listens to.
