# ISSUE 1 — "THE FUTURES THAT WEREN'T THERE"
### Admissibility, Constraint, Projection, Reachability
### v2 — page-by-page poster format (supersedes prior panel-script draft)

**Format note:** Skimmable-first, readable-second — matches the existing
poster style, not a sequential-panel comic script. Each page should work as a
standalone image before the reader engages with any caption text.

**The through-line:** one road, shown four times, in four visual registers,
without saying so until the reader notices:
1. Hidden behind the barricade (Page 1)
2. Missing branch of the continuation cone (Page 3)
3. Missing line on the city map (Page 4, evidence card)
4. Rediscovered pavement, walked in person (Page 5)

The theory and the mystery resolve in the same instant the reader realizes
these were never four images. They were one road, seen through Constraint,
Projection, and Reachability in turn.

---

## PAGE 1 — THE MYSTERY

**Top banner** (full width): THE FUTURES THAT WEREN'T THERE — Admissibility,
Constraint, Projection, Reachability

**Main art:** Rain-soaked city intersection. Barricade. Dead-end sign. The
Detective staring into darkness beyond it. Faint ghost-outlines of roads
just barely visible past the barricade — first appearance of the road.

**Panel A:** City official — *"Nothing back there. Never was."*
**Panel B:** Close on the Detective — *"'Never was' is a claim about
history."*
**Panel C:** The road vanishing into dark, ghost lines faint.

**Bottom banner:** CASE FILE #1: A FUTURE HAS GONE MISSING

---

## PAGE 2 — TWO WITNESSES

**Left half:** E, diner booth, red tie, blueprints and street maps spread out.
Caption block: EXECUTABLE COST — *"I only care whether you can get there."*

**Right half:** R, same booth, blue tie, documents and city records. Caption
block: REPAIR COST — *"I care whether anyone still recognizes it."*

**Center panel:** The Detective's notebook, handwritten:
> Reachable?
> Recognized?
> Not the same question.

**Bottom banner:** TWO WITNESSES. TWO DIFFERENT KINDS OF DISTANCE.

---

## PAGE 3 — THE FAN

**Full page, visual centerpiece.** The intersection opens into the
continuation cone Γ(x) — blue street network fanning outward from a single
point. Half lit and legible. Half crosshatched into near-erasure. The
Detective walks a single crosshatched branch — *second appearance of the
road, now as a missing branch of the cone.*

Small labels within the art: OPEN FUTURES / UNCOUNTED FUTURES / CLOSED
FUTURES — three distinct shadings, not two, so the reader can already see
that "gone" is not one category.

**Bottom caption:**
> A future doesn't have to be destroyed to disappear.
> It only has to stop being counted.

---

## PAGE 4 — THE INVESTIGATION BOARD

**Full page,** classic detective corkboard: strings, photos, maps, torn
documents. Three evidence cards, each large enough to read at a glance:

**CONSTRAINT** — concrete barrier, broken bridge, locked gate.
Card caption: *The road is gone.*

**PROJECTION** — a city map with a neighborhood erased, next to an aerial
photo where the same road is still clearly visible. *Third appearance of the
road, now as a missing line on an official map that contradicts the aerial
photo sitting right next to it.*
Card caption: *The map forgot.*

**RECORD DAMAGE** — burned archive box, missing survey page, corrupted
database readout.
Card caption: *Nobody knows which happened.*

**Bottom banner:** THESE LOOK IDENTICAL FROM FAR AWAY.

---

## PAGE 5 — WALKING THE TERRITORY

**Large vertical panel:** The Detective, flashlight raised, physically
walking the forgotten road. Overgrown pavement, an old street sign emerging
from vines. *Fourth appearance of the road — rediscovered, in person, no
longer an abstraction.*

**Small insets, side by side:**
Map says: ❌ No road.
Reality says: ✔ Road exists.

**Final panel, page bottom:** The Detective at the far end of the road, city
skyline distant behind him.
> Caption: Reachability lives in the territory, not the paperwork.

---

## PAGE 6 — THE CONFRONTATION

**Panel A:** The official, defensive, arms crossed.
**Panel B:** The Detective laying out the evidence cards from Page 4 on a
table between them.
**Panel C:** E and R standing just behind him, like expert witnesses called
to testify.

**Visual centerpiece:** A large scale, off-balance. One side reads REACHABLE
(weighted down, full). Other side reads ADMISSIBLE (light, nearly empty).
The imbalance is the point — the city never actually weighed these against
each other; it just let one side quietly go empty.

**Detective:**
> "Closing a future is a choice. Forgetting that you made the choice is the
> problem."

---

## PAGE 7 — THE FRAMEWORK (manifesto page)

**Large central figure:** The Detective facing the reader directly. Behind
him: the continuation cone, the city map, the lighthouse on the horizon —
every motif introduced so far, gathered into one image.

**Three giant icons, arranged in a row:**
- CONSTRAINT — road physically blocked.
- PROJECTION — road erased from the map.
- REACHABILITY — road actually exists, underneath both.

**Giant text, full width:** ADMISSIBILITY REQUIRES TELLING THESE APART.

**Bottom strip:** small icons — a city, a codebase, a brain (memory), an
institution, a language — arranged beneath the same three-icon framework,
signaling this triad is about to recur across every future issue.

---

## PAGE 8 — RESOLUTION

**Top panel:** Morning light. Barricade removed. Work crew reopening the
road.

**Middle panel:** The city map, redrawn — the street restored, but dashed
rather than solid. Meaning: back on the table, not yet decided.

**Large final panel:** The Lighthouse, sea, sunrise. The once-forgotten
street now visible in the foreground, leading toward it.

**Final caption:**
> Nothing was destroyed. Something was merely uncounted long enough to be
> mistaken for gone.

**Footer:** IT WAS NEVER A DEAD END. IT WAS AN UNCOUNTED FUTURE.

## Production notes

- The four-form road motif is the spine of the issue — do not caption it
  explicitly anywhere. The reader should notice on their own, ideally
  somewhere around Page 5 or on a re-read, that Pages 1, 3, 4, and 5 have
  shown the same physical road throughout. If it needs to be said out loud
  anywhere, it belongs in back-matter or a creator's note, never in-panel.
- Page 7's three-icon framework (Constraint / Projection / Reachability) is
  the shared vocabulary every later issue will assume without
  re-explaining. Keep the icon designs fixed from this point forward — later
  issues can reuse them literally, e.g. small versions in a corner panel,
  rather than re-describing the concepts.
- E and R stay as captioned witnesses here (diner booth, defined by role
  labels rather than dialogue-heavy banter) — reserve the boxing-ring
  staging exclusively for Issue 0 so it doesn't dilute with reuse.
- The off-balance scale on Page 6 is meant to visually rhyme with Issue 0's
  boxing ring — two things in opposition, one clearly winning by default
  rather than by argument.
