# ISSUE 11 — "THE WITNESS WHO NEVER CHANGED HER STORY"
### Persistence Before Truth
### Page-by-page poster format, matching Issues 1–10

**Case type:** The most direct callback in the series to Issue 0. That
issue's closing line was "what survives every faithful witness is two
numbers per ordered pair." This issue takes that phrase out of metaphor and
puts it in an actual courtroom, with actual witnesses, and asks the same
question about testimony that Issue 0 asked about states.

**The claim this issue argues against:** that truth is what matches a
single external check — a witness's story is "true" because it lined up,
once, with the crime-scene photo. **Persistence before truth** argues the
opposite: correspondence to one snapshot is weak evidence. What survives
being asked again, differently, by someone new, years apart, unprompted —
that's the stronger claim on reality.

**The loss — or rather, the reopened case:** A conviction rested heavily on
one eyewitness whose account matched the crime-scene photo closely at
trial. A second witness's account, vaguer at the time and never checked
against the photo the same way, was given far less weight. Years later, the
first witness's story has started contradicting itself under new
questioning. The second witness's account, tested independently five
separate times by five different interviewers, hasn't moved an inch.

**The four-form motif:** one small, specific detail from the second
witness's account, shown identically four times —
1. In her original, under-weighted 1998 statement (Page 1)
2. Recovered unchanged in a completely independent interview years later,
   different interviewer, different question order (Page 3)
3. Cross-checked against three more independent interviews on the
   evidence board, word for word consistent (Page 4)
4. Tested live, on the page, with a brand-new line of questioning nobody's
   used before — and it holds (Page 5)

---

## PAGE 1 — THE MYSTERY

**Main art:** A courthouse archive room, two case files open side by side.
Witness A's file: thick, a photograph paperclipped to the front, "MATCHES
SCENE PHOTO — CONFIRMED" stamped across it. Witness B's file: thin, a
sticky note reading "uncertain, low confidence" from the original
detective.

**Panel A:** The original prosecutor, defensive — *"Her story matched the
photo. That's what confirmed it. That's the whole job of evidence."*
**Panel B:** Close on the Detective, holding both files — *"Matching a
photo once tells you she was consistent with one snapshot on one day. It
doesn't tell you she'd survive being asked again."*
**Panel C:** Close on Witness B's sticky note — "uncertain" — sitting on
top of an account nobody's actually managed to shake since.

**Bottom banner:** CASE FILE #11: THE WITNESS EVERYONE TRUSTED LEAST HAS
NEVER ONCE CHANGED HER STORY

---

## PAGE 2 — TWO KINDS OF EVIDENCE

**Panel A:** A retired detective, holding up the crime-scene photo next to
Witness A's original statement, edges aligned — *"They matched. That's
correspondence. That's what proof looks like."*
**Panel B:** A legal researcher, five separate interview transcripts fanned
out, spanning a decade — *"This is what proof looks like when you ask
twice. Five times, actually. Different people asking, years apart, and it
never moved."*
**Center caption:**
> CORRESPONDENCE: does it match one fixed thing?
> PERSISTENCE: does it survive being asked again, by someone new, who
> wasn't trying to get the same answer?
> These are not the same test, and they don't always agree.

**Bottom banner:** ONE MATCH IS A COINCIDENCE UNTIL PROVEN OTHERWISE. FIVE
INDEPENDENT SURVIVALS ARE HARDER TO EXPLAIN AWAY.

---

## PAGE 3 — WHAT SURVIVES EVERY FAITHFUL WITNESS

**Full page, visual centerpiece.** Direct callback to Issue 0's staging:
the boxing ring reused one more time, but empty this time — instead, two
testimony-shapes rendered the way Issue 0 rendered E and R. Witness A's
account: a single sharp point, precisely matched to the photo, but shown
with faint ghost-lines radiating outward showing how easily it moved under
each new round of questioning. Witness B's account: a small, stable knot,
shown identically across five overlaid transcript-layers, barely shifting
at all. *Second appearance of the detail — the one specific fact, glowing
identically in all five of Witness B's overlaid layers.*

**Sidebar:**
- MONOTONE UNDER REDESCRIPTION — a story that survives no matter who asks,
  in what order, how much later.
- FRAGILE UNDER REDESCRIPTION — a story that only held together for the
  one specific way it was originally asked.

**Bottom caption:**
> Issue 0 asked what survives every faithful redescription of a system.
> This is what it looks like when the system being redescribed is a memory,
> and the redescriptions are cross-examinations.

---

## PAGE 4 — THE INVESTIGATION BOARD

**Full page,** three evidence cards, tracking each witness's account
across time and interviewer:

**WITNESS A, FOUR TELLINGS** — the original statement and three later
retellings, laid side by side, visibly diverging — details added, dropped,
reordered, a key detail flatly contradicted between telling two and
telling three.
Card caption: *Matched the photo once. Hasn't matched itself since.*

**WITNESS B, FIVE TELLINGS** — five interview transcripts, different
interviewers, different years, different question orders, the same small
detail present and unchanged in every single one. *Third appearance of the
detail, now formally logged across all five.*
Card caption: *Never checked against a photo. Never needed to be. It kept
surviving the only test that actually counts.*

**THE ORIGINAL WEIGHTING** — the court's original scoring sheet, giving
Witness A's "photo match" ten points and Witness B's "uncertain" a
one-point footnote.
Card caption: *The court measured the wrong kind of evidence, and measured
it confidently.*

**Bottom banner:** ONE OF THESE WITNESSES WAS ALWAYS BEING RE-VERIFIED.
NOBODY WAS COUNTING.

---

## PAGE 5 — WALKING THE TERRITORY

**Large vertical panel:** The Detective, running a brand-new interview with
both witnesses, using a question sequence nobody in the case file has ever
used before — testing both accounts against a genuinely novel
redescription. *Fourth appearance of the detail — asked about sideways,
unexpectedly, years after the fact, and still, from Witness B, exactly the
same.*

**Small insets:**
Witness A, new questioning: account shifts again, a new inconsistency
appears.
Witness B, new questioning: same detail, same order, same certainty as
1998.

**Final panel:** The Detective, closing his notebook.
> Caption: A story that only holds up the way it was first asked was never
> that strong to begin with. A story that holds up no matter how you ask
> it was strong the whole time. Nobody was measuring for that.

---

## PAGE 6 — THE CONFRONTATION

**Panel A:** The original prosecutor, still unconvinced — *"The photo
matched. What more proof does anyone need?"*
**Panel B:** The Detective — *"A photo is one faithful witness. You need
more than one before you can call something invariant. You had five,
sitting in a drawer marked 'uncertain,' this whole time."*
**Panel C:** Witness B herself, quiet, unsurprised — she's been telling the
same story for a decade and nobody asked why that mattered.

**Visual centerpiece:** the two testimony-shapes from Page 3 again, this
time both drawn to the same scale, side by side — Witness A's sharp point
surrounded by scatter, Witness B's small stable knot, barely moved at all.

**Detective:**
> "You built your whole case on the witness who matched a photograph once.
> You should have built it on the witness nobody could shake, no matter
> how many times, how many ways, how many years apart you asked her."

---

## PAGE 7 — THE FRAMEWORK (manifesto page)

**Large central figure:** The Detective, the two testimony-shapes from
Page 3 behind him, drawn now exactly the way Issue 0 drew E and R — but
unlabeled as E and R, this time labeled CORRESPONDENCE and PERSISTENCE.

**Icons, row:**
- CORRESPONDENCE — a single match to one fixed point.
- PERSISTENCE — survival across many independent, faithful retellings.
- INVARIANCE — what's left once you've asked every way you can think to
  ask.

**Giant text:** TRUTH IS NOT WHAT MATCHES ONE DESCRIPTION. IT IS WHAT
SURVIVES EVERY FAITHFUL RETELLING.

**Small line, explicit callback:**
> Issue 0 said it about states: what survives every faithful witness is
> the cost to get there, and the cost to get back. This is the same
> theorem, wearing a courtroom instead of a directed graph. The math
> doesn't care which one you ask it about.

**Bottom strip:** five domain icons, each now shown with a small "asked
five times, unchanged" motif — a repeated, stable dot pattern — extending
persistence-before-truth to institutions, memory, and language, not just
testimony.

---

## PAGE 8 — RESOLUTION

**Top panel:** The retrial, Witness B's account finally given its actual
evidentiary weight, five independent interviews entered as a body rather
than one dismissed footnote.

**Middle panel:** A revised scoring guideline for the court itself — not
"does it match one piece of physical evidence," but "how many independent,
faithful retellings has it survived."

**Large final panel:** The Lighthouse, its beam this time shown returning
to the exact same patch of water, over and over, unprompted, each pass
identical to the last — persistence rendered as literal repetition rather
than a single flash.

**Final caption:**
> Nothing here needed a photograph to be true. It needed to survive being
> asked again. It always had. Nobody had been counting how many times.

**Footer:** SHE NEVER MATCHED A PHOTOGRAPH. SHE NEVER ONCE CHANGED HER
STORY EITHER.

## Production notes

- Page 3 and Page 7 should both echo Issue 0's boxing-ring staging visually
  even though the ring itself is empty or replaced — the point is for a
  returning reader to recognize the *shape* of Issue 0's argument
  immediately, the same way Issue 3 rhymed its scale with Issue 1's.
- Keep the original prosecutor sympathetic and procedurally reasonable —
  "the photo matched" is a real, standard evidentiary practice, not
  negligence. The issue's argument is that correspondence-checking is
  weaker than persistence-checking, not that the prosecutor broke any
  rule that existed at the time.
- The detail that recurs four times (Page 1, 3, 4, 5) should be small and
  mundane by design — same discipline as Issue 6's groove and Issue 9's
  phrase. If it's dramatic or plot-critical on its own, the point becomes
  about the detail instead of about the pattern of its survival.
