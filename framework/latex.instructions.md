# LaTeX essay instructions

- Follow `.github/copilot-instructions.md` and the relevant essay section of `RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md`.
- Use `\claimnum{key}` or an approved generated semantic macro for empirical and derived quantitative claims. Do not appropriate `\num` from `siunitx`.
- Do not insert a literal result merely to satisfy a missing key. Repair or add the owning check.
- Cite only entries in `bib/series.bib`. Do not invent authors, titles, venues, years, DOIs, URLs, page numbers, or access dates.
- Use precise conditional language. Identify whether a result is reported, replotted, reconstructed, simulated, consistency-checked, or independently certified.
- Do not state that a paper is wrong. State what follows under the checked artifact, version, instance, Hamiltonian, metric, and boundary.
- Distinguish proposition, theorem, proof, exact certificate, reconstruction, empirical observation, interpretation, conjecture, and analogy.
- Attribute formulations reused from earlier essays. Attribute “Valuation is digital; chance is magnetic” to *The Shape of Search*.
- Avoid em dashes and first-person narration.
- Keep quotations short and necessary. Preserve exact wording when the term itself is being analyzed.
- Every figure and table caption states its evidential status and source version.
- Do not use page targets as a reason to repeat an argument or manufacture sections.
- Build the modified essay and run the numeric-claim and citation checks before declaring completion.
